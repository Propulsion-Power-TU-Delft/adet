# Models

Catalogue of the deviation and loss models implemented in ADeT, grouped by machine type, then by the physical mechanism. Each mechanism page gives a table of its models with the machine type they apply to (axial, radial, mixed or any), followed by the equations, source location and citation for every model.

Conventions: subscript $0$ is the inlet and $1$ the outlet of the row or component; $r^{r}$ denotes relative (rothalpy-based) quantities; $\beta_m$ is the metal angle, $\beta$ the flow angle, $\alpha$ the absolute flow angle; $\Delta s$ is the specific entropy generation and $\Delta h$ an enthalpy loss.
